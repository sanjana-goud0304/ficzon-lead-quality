# Phase 6c: agent comparison within comparable segments

_Generated by `scripts/run_agent_analysis.py`. Train + validation months only (Apr-Sep 2018)._

Each lead is compared with other agents' leads in the same Delivery_Mode x Location x month stratum (leave-agent-out). `gap_pp` = observed High rate minus the rate expected from those peers, in percentage points, with a 95% bootstrap interval. Leads in strata with fewer than 20 peer leads are excluded, and agents with fewer than 30 comparable leads get no estimate.

**What this can and cannot say.** A positive gap means the agent's leads reached High Potential more often than comparable leads handled by others. It does not separate better selling from a different habit of recording Status, and it only adjusts for mode, location and month. Leads may also differ in ways these fields do not capture.

- Comparable share of leads: 81% overall; agents routed to segments few others handled have a low share and a less reliable estimate.
