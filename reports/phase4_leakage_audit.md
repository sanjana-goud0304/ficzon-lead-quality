## 6. Segment overlap: Product, Location, Delivery_Mode

| segment                    |    n |   pct_junk_or_not_responding |   pct_high |
|:---------------------------|-----:|-----------------------------:|-----------:|
| All leads                  | 6039 |                         37.2 |       36.7 |
| Location = Other Locations | 2002 |                         60.2 |       15.8 |
| Location = Bangalore       | 1734 |                         21.6 |       58.3 |
| Product = P15              | 1249 |                         76.5 |        4.6 |
| Delivery_Mode = Mode-5     | 2483 |                         51.2 |       24.4 |
| Delivery_Mode = Mode-1     | 2136 |                         26.3 |       47.6 |

- Share of Product P15 leads that are also 'Other Locations': 87.7%
- Share of Product P15 leads that are also Mode-5: 77.8%
- Share of Mode-5 leads that are also 'Other Locations': 49.7%

These three fields overlap heavily, so they are not independent signals, and each is concentrated in leads that never engage (Junk / Not Responding). Whether they are known at creation or filled in after contact decides whether they are legitimate predictors or a restatement of the outcome. This needs the project owner.
