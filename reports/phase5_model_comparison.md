## Reading the results

- Best creation_time model (Gradient boosting) has ROC-AUC 0.566 versus 0.541 for the Source-rate rule; their 95% intervals overlap. The creation-safe features do not clearly beat a one-column source lookup.
- The three extended models have ROC-AUC 0.754-0.761; their intervals all overlap, so the model type does not matter much here.
- Extended-set performance depends on Product, Location and Delivery_Mode being known at lead creation (unconfirmed).
- Validation is a single month, and model choice made on it is provisional until Phase 6.
