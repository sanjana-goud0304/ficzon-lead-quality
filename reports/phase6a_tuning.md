# Phase 6a: time-ordered cross-validation and tuning

_Generated by `scripts/run_tuning.py`. Train + validation months only (Apr-Sep 2018); test months were never loaded._

Expanding-window CV: train on all earlier months, validate on 2018-06, 2018-07, 2018-08, 2018-09 (4 folds). Selection metric: mean PR-AUC across folds. Parsimony rule: choose the simplest model class whose best mean PR-AUC is within 0.01 of the overall best (order: logistic regression, gradient boosting, random forest). Thresholds maximize F1 on pooled out-of-fold scores and are frozen before the test months are touched.
